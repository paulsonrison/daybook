# P09 implementation detail

This expands approved P09 (staff compensation, morning handovers and daily cash closing), using the reviewed P08 financial interfaces. Implementation begins after the P08 checkpoint.

1. Add forced-RLS effective-dated pay rules and employment dates, attendance, confirmed advances, approved reimbursable staff expenses, immutable payslips/claims and separately confirmed payouts. No configured rule means unknown pay, not zero. Support salary, per-copy and blended rules; expose route allowance explicitly as an amount per worked route/day.
2. Default salary proration to calendar days, preserving paid leave and excluding recorded unpaid leave/absence. Expose the owner's alternative fixed monthly/no automatic proration policy. Snapshot employment/rule periods and attendance inputs. Round each pay-rule segment once to the nearest paisa (half up), never each day. Restrict unprorated salary rule changes to month boundaries so two full monthly salaries cannot accidentally overlap.
3. Derive commission from actual original fulfilled delivery snapshots, credited once to the staff actually assigned on that date, including substitutes. Replacement copies never earn a second commission. A previously unfulfilled delivery replaced later can earn its first commission on confirmed fulfilment; prior payroll claims remain immutable. Protect posted payroll source attribution; later corrections become explicit current-period adjustments.
4. Preview the complete computation with a fingerprint and expected versions. Post only completed months (or confirmed final employment through today), with acknowledgement of missing attendance/preparation. Claim advances and reimbursements once. Apply advances only against earned compensation, carry unapplied advances forward and keep reimbursements separate. Posting a payslip does not claim funds were disbursed; recording a payout requires exact amount, channel, reference where applicable and agent confirmation evidence.
5. Extend collection attribution without rewriting confirmed payment identity. Record explicit agency-counter versus staff/route collection custody. Historical/unclassified cash receipts stay unknown and require audited classification before cash closing. New financial forms expose the collector choice; never infer historical staff custody from today's live route.
6. Summarize unclaimed confirmed cash receipts by route/staff/day, showing digital and pending receipts separately. Record immutable rapid handovers, actual cash received, linked eligible publisher returns and owner sign-off. Reuse P06 original-lot cost/cutoff/quantity validation inside the same transaction. Returns do not guess which resident delivery failed and do not automatically reduce staff wages.
7. A shortage or surplus creates an agency suspense memo. It does not create staff liability or payroll deduction. Explicit reasoned resolution classifies the memo; any staff payroll adjustment is a separate approved record. Late cash remittances link to the original handover, reduce outstanding suspense once and post actual cash on the receiving day, preserving the original sign-off.
8. Keep staff-held cash separate from agency drawer cash. Expected staff receipts and actual handovers are compared at handover. Drawer inflow counts actual handovers plus explicitly direct agency cash receipts, never the same receipt twice. Include confirmed cash advances, payroll payouts, refunds, petty expenses and bank cash deposits as separate outflows; digital payments do not enter the drawer. Do not count a reimbursement twice.
9. Cash closing requires observed opening/count/deposit amounts, sign-off and reasons for variances. Suggest a prior signed closing balance when available; an absent opening is unknown. Closed days and their source movements are immutable. Late/backdated financial entries need an explicit current-day adjustment instead of silently reopening a signed day. Block close while cash custody or handover outcomes remain unresolved, or explicitly record their suspense classification.
10. Compose staff pay/attendance/advance/payslip and rapid route handover/cash-closing screens from shared UI. Show source counts and exact money, missing/stale/error states, digital-versus-cash separation, sign-off receipts and original-request recovery. No dedicated payroll/handover frames were found by the current inventory search; verify this during P14; use the approved shared foundations without claiming a nonexistent reference match.
11. Observe RED→GREEN tests for proration/leave, substitute attribution, no replacement double commission, advance/reimbursement claims, stale posting, cash custody/digital separation, shortages without wage debit, original-lot returns, signed close, late remittance and callback/request replay. Verify PostgreSQL, Chrome, build/lint/types; obtain independent review, fix and rereview; checkpoint before P10. Merge remains gated on P14.

Ruling: receipt confirmation establishes resident settlement; cash custody separately establishes where physical agency money is held. A receipt and its later handover are one movement into the drawer, not two revenues. Unclassified historical custody must never be invented.

Ruling: route allowance is explicitly configured per worked route/day. Fixed monthly salary is an explicit no-automatic-proration choice; the default calendar-day policy uses employment dates and recorded unpaid absence. The preview displays the selected policy and its inputs before posting; no statutory payroll compliance or deductions are inferred.

Ruling: handover suspense and drawer variance are distinct. The drawer starts from actual money remitted; the original short handover remains an independently auditable suspense balance, preventing duplicate loss recognition or an automatic staff debt.

Interfaces to preserve: P08 payments have immutable confirmation balances, receipt dates versus open-period posting dates, and separate deposit liability. P06 return lots preserve net cost/cutoff. P05 delivery snapshots preserve assigned/permanent staff. P07 paid/closed source records stay frozen. P10 must reuse P08 notification intents and add provider/consent processing without claiming unconfigured delivery.

Implementation rulings: reimbursement entries are approved unpaid claims, not a second confirmed disbursement. Final employment applies across pay segments sharing the same employment start. Midmonth final departure is permitted under the explicit fixed-month salary policy, while ordinary fixed-rate changes retain month boundaries. Known-ended employment can receive correction-only payroll; unknown employment cannot be priced as zero. A signed handover variance itself is the immutable agency suspense memo and never creates a staff wage deduction. Each original publisher return can belong to only one signed handover.
